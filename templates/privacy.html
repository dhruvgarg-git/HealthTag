Privacy and consent

HealthTag is a hackathon prototype. Please do not store real medical data.

What we store

Name, age, blood group, allergies, conditions, medications, medical history, and your emergency contact's name and phone number.

Why

Only to show the right information to the right person in an emergency: bystanders see the contact number, verified responders see the emergency card, and the full history opens only after your emergency contact approves.

How it is protected

Medical fields are encrypted in the database. Every access is logged and shown to you.

Deleting your data

Open your HealthTag dashboard, choose Edit details, and use "Delete this HealthTag". Everything linked to it is permanently removed.
